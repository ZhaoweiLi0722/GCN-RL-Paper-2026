the same frozen model or continued R4 imitation remains unmeasured.

and that complete comparison. It does not reopen either consumed attempt,
repeat qualification, load a research tensor, simulate a patient world or fit

## Validation and handoff

Mock end-to-end execution covers loading, identical forks, preflight clone
restoration, continuation, sealing, final evaluation, raw verification and
local archive. Real Adam/SGD steps are forbidden in fixtures; metadata-only
optimizer doubles are not scientific fitting. Restore checks retain live
irreversible budgets and failure closes the mock attempt.

Two fixture-only issues were corrected: macOS temporary-directory alias
resolution and an artificial saved-state dictionary key. No scientific attempt
failed or started during this preparation. Independent admission fixtures also
found an environment-construction phase gap; the implementation now rejects
episode builds in binding, seal, analysis, archive and closure phases.

Final combined suite: **120 tests passed in 40.539 seconds**. The suite includes
11 recovery integration cases, 27 independent admission cases and the affected
frozen campaign, raw-reader, saved-loader and watchdog regressions. Full-repo
`compileall -q .` passed. No real numerical optimizer was used in these tests.
Final freeze identities are recorded in the Live checkpoint and `workflow.json`.

One complete decision remains: approve this source-frozen continuation and
evaluation packet once, with the full limits above. Routine progression inside
that approval would not require further per-phase permission. Until then, the
visible automation remains paused and there is no running scientific process.
Passing qualification, engineering tests or the approval itself is not evidence
of RL gain, clinical noninferiority, deployment adaptation or publishability.
