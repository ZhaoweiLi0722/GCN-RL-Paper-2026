The distinct bounded-message study reports 4.843% lower GCN cost than its
same-information, same-budget non-graph comparator, with 16.667 additional
patient losses and 12.375 fewer completions. All three block cost means improve,
and all three block loss means worsen. This cost-only architecture signal is
not transferred to the full-controller result or called patient equivalence.

The two approximately 4.84% figures have different denominators and protocols.
Historical Flat and stronger old models are retained, not replaced by an
information-poor control. Latest failed GCN/GATv2 recipient and joint-action
directions are disclosed. No experiment was run for this manuscript revision.

## Files and reproduction boundary

- `main.tex` is the existing manuscript, revised in place.
- `main.pdf` is its 41-page collaborator-review rendering, not a submission.
- `sections/related-work.tex` and `references-current.bib` restore the relevant
  literature synthesis; the historical bibliography is not overwritten.
- `figures/review-*.pdf` are vector figures used by the manuscript. Companion
  PNGs permit inspection without a LaTeX toolchain.
- `data/` contains selected fields from existing comparison files and preserved
  readouts. `data/source-manifest.json` records original source hashes and bytes.
  Source paths are provenance identifiers, not an assertion that every source
  object is present in this scoped branch.
- `scripts/build_review_figures.py` rebuilds the five figures using only packaged
  data. Python and Matplotlib are required. Run it without `--export` from any
  working directory. It imports no simulator or model and performs no bootstrap,
  fitting, new scoring, or formal scientific comparison.
- `--export` is only for the local coordinator with the preserved result roots;
  it is not necessary for a collaborator to rebuild the figures.

This is not a full runnable release of training inputs, simulator snapshots,
models, or archives. It does not resolve the prior full-source-access gap.
Human source verification, declarations, and scientific approval remain pending.
The manuscript is for collaborator review, not journal submission.
Historical diff helpers and title-page templates are not the current review
document; the entry point is `main.tex` and its matching `main.pdf`.
