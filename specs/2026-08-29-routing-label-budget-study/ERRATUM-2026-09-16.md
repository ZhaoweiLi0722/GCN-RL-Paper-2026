# Erratum (2026-09-16)

The study's `state_generation.decision_epochs` were 30, 55, and 80. The routing
environment's episode horizon is 52 epochs, and stepping past `done` continues
to advance the clock, so states snapshotted at epochs 55 and 80 are post-
terminal: their continuation rollouts end after one step. In the pool on disk,
mean remaining cost is 1,411M at epoch 30 versus 73.5M at epoch 55 and 66.7M
at epoch 80. Eighteen of the 27 states are therefore degenerate.

Consequences: the reported 0.821 best-action agreement at G1's 3-versus-5
budget, the agreement curve, and the allocation comparison are dominated by
one-step labels and must not be cited. The classification
`precondition_violated_cannot_adjudicate_g1` stands for a different reason
than stated. The original results file is left unchanged as the record.

Corrected measurement on in-horizon states (epochs 4 to 44, 192 states, MDL-2
anchor): agreement 0.51 at k = 1, 0.56 at k = 2, 0.61 at k = 4, 0.68 at k = 8.
See `specs/2026-09-16-routing-headroom-anchor-screen/results.md`.
