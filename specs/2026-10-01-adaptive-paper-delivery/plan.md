### First experiment package

[Pilot budget draft](pilot-budget-draft.md) and its JSON enumerate the proposed
first package. The initial design is three independent training blocks, 32
full episodes per continued arm, and 12 fresh paired worlds per block for five
controllers. The old 19,344-step arithmetic excludes startup and is NOT a full
execution budget. D3 must count every qualification/preflight/clone call too.
The new draft now enumerates 22,224 main env.step calls, 1,920 optimizer calls
and a six-hour global cap including setup/readback/preservation. An optional
72-step appendix is disabled by default and is not part of the recommended
main packet. These are proposed ceilings, not approved or measured use. The
actual new runner, exact initialization and seed-collision inventory still
need completion before the single approval request.

Use one policy design, not a head/width/learning-rate sweep. Retain the current
52-step scenario and raw absolute-cost objective initially. Fix initialization
competency prospectively using independent qualification data. Seal the same
actor/value/optimizer/RNG start into its frozen/PPO/BC forks; reset semantics
and which components continue learning must be explicit. Never compare an
untrained new actor to a strong baseline and call its easy recovery RL value.

The baseline intervention must use training data and action-independent public
state/time only, never test returns or evaluator oracle Q. Freeze the fitting
schedule, target scale and handling of policy-dependent sampling in D3. An
independent value model is not automatically an adequate baseline; diagnose it
from the single recorded run, without tuning against its test outcomes.

Primary contrast: final PPO minus its own frozen policy in raw total cost on
paired worlds. Also compare PPO with BC-CONTINUE to distinguish interaction
learning from extra optimization/imitation. Report each seed, patient loss,
completions, waiting, expiry, active terminal obligations, requested and executed
action differences and compute use. Declare the deterministic or stochastic
deployment mode before execution. No best-checkpoint selection after testing.
Three seeds are development evidence; many paired rows are not many trained
policies. Practical and patient-outcome tolerances must be written before
confirmation; missing domain justification remains missing, not a zero-risk claim.

## Reward and scenario decisions

Do not modify reward simply because improvement is null. Keep separate:
numerical scaling, variance reduction/credit assignment, and a change in the
scientific objective. Correct sign/units do not establish clinical validity.

If a genuine accounting or objective mismatch is established, stop affected
science and propose a repair before spending the pilot budget. If delayed credit
alone is the suspected issue, prefer a single justified baseline/return-design
comparison while preserving raw evaluation costs. Any potential-based shaping
proposal must specify finite-horizon terminal handling and no test-data fitting.
For cost/service conflict, consider an explicitly constrained or Pareto study
with independently justified limits; for deferred workload, define lifecycle
closure or terminal value without double counting. These are separate designs,
not edits made during an ongoing run.

An unknown persistent facility-capacity change is the first scenario candidate;
lead-time shifts are second. Neither is automatically ready. Require a genuine
controllable effect, plausible persistence, observable feedback and calibrated
or explicitly synthetic assumptions before training. E1's missing field data
stay missing. If deployment adaptation becomes a claim, compare online-updated
and frozen history policies with identical information, adaptive rules and
online system identification plus MPC. A reactive fixed controller is not
online parameter learning. Do not add four scenarios at once just to find a win.
