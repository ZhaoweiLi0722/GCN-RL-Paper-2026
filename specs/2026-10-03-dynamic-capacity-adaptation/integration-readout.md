## Delivered Real Binding

- `capacity_native.py` binds the unchanged synthetic proposal to the native
  patient/support host. Immutable identity-keyed arrival, patient-attribute
  and efficiency tapes are paired across arms. Native construction/reset/step
  are admitted individually; resource clipping is a terminal failure. No
  fractional patients, biological acceleration or hidden efficiency input.
- `capacity_completion_control.py` supplies the public per-ID censored
  interval filter, fixed proximal adaptive rule and 16-sequence approximate
  ID-MPC. Native patient environment clones are never used by the planner.
  Filter updates retain work intervals across priority changes and transfers.
  The forecast is an approximation, not an exact posterior or optimal control.
- `capacity_ddpg.py` and `capacity_ddpg_learner.py` supply separate fresh
  float32 GCN actor/critic/targets, BC, critic warmup, DDPG, executed-hours
  replay and full optimizer/RNG/replay snapshots. No historical actor loads.
- `capacity_public_features.py` fixes unfitted per-site operational, patient
  summary, 8-receipt history, pending labor and shared-filter features. It takes
  only the common typed public view. The GCN ring uses normalized self loops;
  this is not an isolated graph-architecture attribution experiment.
- `capacity_pilot_runner.py` supplies the actual serial entry: full settled
  teacher trajectories, initialization, offline policy training, all three
  model seals, then the six same-start deployment arms. The last control row
  includes every tail cost before its already-budgeted update. No tail neural
  actions, hidden qualification, test checkpoint selection or resampling.
- `capacity_pilot_resources.py` persists pre-call receipts for native/neural/
  optimizer operations. Model/filter work reserves exactly384/100 calls before
  each complete decision/receipt and accounts each primitive before dispatch.
  Unfinished reservations remain consumed and are not falsely called executed.
  Wall time includes serialization, recording and archive verification.
- `capacity_pilot_execution.py` binds committed source/runtime/input/seed
  locks, exclusive non-overwriting claim and an external phase/global watchdog.
  `run_dynamic_capacity_pilot.py` only exposes freeze, launch and owned-child
  modes. No retry or resume mode exists.
- `capacity_pilot_analysis.py` independently reads compressed raw costs and
  public patient registries, reconstructs all comparisons and the fixed
  descriptive bootstrap, and explicitly marks missing secondary fields. It
  does not import a scientific model or simulator.

## Preservation And Interpretation

Raw64-epoch records include patient identity/status/survival, requested and
executed effort, additive cost components, receipt events and current public
input. Private host/tape/RNG state is separately preserved, never a policy
input. Evaluation snapshots reference the immutable offline replay in their
block seal rather than duplicating it108times; complete world state is saved.
No saved scientific state may be resumed under this one-attempt authorization.

The fixed common tail purchases the public shortfall for live support/ready
patients through epoch60, starts no new transfers/arrivals/flexible labor, and
honors already incurred obligations. All64epochs are charged even when empty.
Unsettled IDs, unaccounted stock, or pending obligations end the trial; no forced
writeoff, extra tail or invented terminal benefit.

The first budgeted teacher trajectory is the actual native preflight and is
retained, not duplicated. Artificial validation is not scientific execution.
The combined87focused tests passed; subsequent recording additions passed all
33affected checks. Whole-tree compileall passed. Tests guard real native
construction and optimizer.step; their fake tokens are never reported as fits.
Raw recording also includes actual controller/native/filter wall durations and
private simulator productive/idle total-hour audit, explicitly excluded from
controller information. Ordinary versus flexible productive-hour attribution
is not identified; do not invent that split. Paired executed-hour differences
are recorded outcomes, not additional greedy-model evaluations.
The proposal's original draft false flags remain unchanged; only a subsequent
committed effective authorization can admit this exact trial. Previous
scientific failures and null results stay closed, unchanged and available.

Synthetic E1 assumptions remain uncalibrated. The target is online incremental
cost/patient benefit beyond equally informed frozen/adaptive control. Positive
training loss or any improvement over a weak fixed allocation is not sufficient.
The protocol does not establish publication acceptance or guarantee RL benefit.
