## Constraints Carried Forward

- **Primary constraint:** an unknown persistent coefficient does not itself
  justify online RL. Any online-weight claim needs benefit over its matched
  frozen history-aware fork with the same updated public information, alongside
  an adaptive rule and online identification plus MPC. Charge interaction,
  exploration, model queries and computation fairly. Graph benefit is a
  separate attribution question, not implied by an adaptation gain.
- Change the commitment-to-usable-capacity relationship, not merely demand
  severity, announced schedules or stock availability. Hide neither genuinely
  public measurements nor supply the latent coefficient/future change time.
  Throughput can be censored by workload, reagents and downstream queues;
  same information rules do not imply identical trajectories after actions
  diverge. See [next-study decision sheet][decision-sheet], Required Operational
  Inputs and Attribution That Must Survive.
- Keep the physical meaning explicit. Committed patient source still floors
  starts at `src/env/patient_capacity_planning.py:329`; continuous actor output
  is not continuous patient production. The separate work primitive at
  `src/env/service_effort_mechanics.py:97` accumulates work and charges effort,
  while `src/baselines/public_service_response.py:27` handles censored receipts.
  None establishes labor-driven biological acceleration or a useful clinical
  value gradient. Without operational evidence, label the extension synthetic.
- Set coherent conservation and terminal accounting before interpreting cost.
  Historical transfer clipping remains at `src/env/capacity_planning.py:487`;
  its effect on old formal comparisons is unknown. The work fixture's
  `src/env/service_effort_mechanics.py:162` explicitly marks its terminal ledger
  unsettled. Preserve historical source; do not gain by deferring liabilities.
- A restricted grid, finite-tree optimum, few worlds, or three training blocks
  cannot establish universal saturation, clinical safety or graph superiority.
  Keep independent evaluation separate from development selection, retain
  adverse patient endpoints, and do not recycle closed holdouts or thresholds.
